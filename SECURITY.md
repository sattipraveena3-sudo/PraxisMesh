# Security policy

PraxisMesh is an early research prototype and has not completed an external security audit.

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to the repository owner. Include the affected version, reproduction steps, impact and any suggested mitigation. Do not include live credentials, personal data or exploit traffic against systems you do not own.

Until production-grade identity, isolation and secret management are implemented, do not connect PraxisMesh to high-impact infrastructure or sensitive data.
